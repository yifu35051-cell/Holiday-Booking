Section 2. Case study and related tasks with marking scheme
You are tasked with designing a Holiday Resort Booking System. The system allows customers to
search for available rooms, book rooms, and make payments. The holiday resort management staff can
add or delete rooms to/from the system, update room availability, and view customer bookings. Based
on the scenario, the core components of the system therefore include:
Customer/member: a holiday resort customer/member is a person who will pay /has paid the holiday
resort to stay there. A member will enjoy some discount, compared to a customer. The
customer/member can search for rooms, book them, and make payments. There is a registration
process for a customer to become a member, with member fees to be paid.
Room: represents a resort room, with relevant information such as room type, price and availability.
Booking: Handles the customer’s/member’s booking details. A reminder is sent 1                     -day earlier to remind
the customer via email.
Payment: Processes payments for the booked rooms or membership fees.
Holiday Resort Management: allows management staff to update room availability and view customer
bookings.
